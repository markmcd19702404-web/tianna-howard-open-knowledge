The series

Tianna Howard Adventures

The Tianna Howard Adventures are historical fiction mysteries by M.L. Woodward for readers aged 11–14, combining real history and global locations with codes, hidden evidence and fictional investigations.

Historical fiction without magic

Each book sends Tianna into a real historical puzzle. It might begin with a lost object, a code or a disputed record.

Tianna travels through real places and searches archives for clues she can trust. She relies on observation and logic rather than supernatural powers.

Audience
