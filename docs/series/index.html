The series

Tianna Howard Adventures

The Tianna Howard Adventures are character-led historical mysteries for readers aged 9–13, following Tianna through global searches rooted in real places and unresolved history.

Tianna follows the clues adults miss

Each published adventure begins with a personal mystery that pulls Tianna into unfinished history, from the search for her missing parents to the trail of a lost Renaissance sculpture.

She travels through real places, studies records and tests the clues she can trust. Historical questions deepen the story, while Tianna’s choices drive it.
