The Tianna Howard Adventures are historical fiction mysteries by M.L. Woodward for readers aged 11–14, combining real history and global locations with codes, hidden evidence and fictional investigations.

The series

History becomes the trail

Tianna follows evidence through real places and disputed records. She uses careful observation and logic rather than fantasy or magic.

Book 1 · published

Tianna and the Mystery of the Beale Treasure

A fast-paced historical adventure inspired by the legendary Beale ciphers, following twelve-year-old Tianna Howard on an international trail of codes, danger and discovery.

Book 2 · published

Tianna and Michelangelo’s Mask of a Faun

A wartime art mystery inspired by the real Mask of a Faun, a Renaissance sculpture attributed to the young Michelangelo and missing since 1944.

Book 3 · in development

Tianna and the Lost Inca Gold

An upcoming historical adventure inspired by Atahualpa’s ransom and the legendary lost Inca treasure of the Llanganates Mountains.

Book 4 · planned

Tianna and the Vanishing Crown Jewels

A planned historical mystery inspired by the still-unsolved disappearance of the Irish Crown Jewels from Dublin Castle in 1907.
