Choose the right mystery for a curious reader

Both published books are historical adventures for ages 11–14. There is no magic. Each mystery begins with real history and clearly separates the evidence from Tianna's fictional investigation.

For readers who enjoy codes

Start with the Beale mystery

Book 1 follows a coded journal and an international trail inspired by the disputed Beale treasure story. It is the best starting point for a new reader.

For readers drawn to art history

Follow the missing faun

Book 2 follows wartime records from Oxford to Florence, inspired by a sculpture attributed to Michelangelo that remains missing.
