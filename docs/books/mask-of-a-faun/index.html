Tianna and Michelangelo’s Mask of a Faun

A lost Renaissance sculpture. A trail of wartime records. A secret that did not end in 1944.

Thirteen-year-old Tianna Howard follows the mystery from Oxford to Florence, uncovering hidden connections between a lost sculpture, coded evidence and people determined to keep the past buried.

The history behind the fiction

A Renaissance marble attributed to Michelangelo that was removed from Castle of Poppi during German looting in 1944.

Historical status: The real work remains missing as of 2026-09-01.

Fiction boundary: Tianna, her investigation and the story's resolution are fictional.

Choosing a Christmas gift for a curious reader?

For parents and grandparents choosing for ages 11–14, this historical mystery connects an adventure in Florence with questions about a missing work of art. Tianna's investigation is fiction; the historical background can be explored separately.
