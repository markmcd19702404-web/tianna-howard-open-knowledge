The author

M.L. Woodward

M.L. Woodward writes fast-paced historical adventures built around real mysteries, places and events.

The Tianna Howard Adventures combine global journeys, research-based history and clever problem-solving, encouraging young readers to question evidence and explore the stories behind the past.

Rather than relying on magic or supernatural powers, the stories challenge Tianna to observe carefully, think logically and decide whom she can trust.
