The continuing search for stolen art

The WWII Art Heist That Still Isn’t Over

How Nazi organizations stole cultural objects across occupied Europe and why the search for missing works continues.

Reviewed 12 September 2026

What the video explains

Nazi organizations systematically seized paintings, sculptures and private collections across occupied Europe. Allied investigators recovered many objects, but provenance research and restitution work continue.

Evidence boundary: A missing object is not automatically confirmed as Nazi-looted. Each claim depends on documentary provenance and the history of the individual work.
