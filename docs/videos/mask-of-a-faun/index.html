Michelangelo and a missing sculpture

Finding the World's Most Famous Missing Sculpture

The real history of Michelangelo’s Mask of a Faun, a Renaissance sculpture missing since the Second World War.

Reviewed 12 September 2026

What the video explains

The Mask of a Faun is traditionally linked to the young Michelangelo. The sculpture was taken from the Castle of Poppi during German looting in 1944 and has not been securely recovered.

Evidence boundary: The real sculpture remains missing. Any recovery or resolution within the Tianna Howard novel is fictional.
