The story begins with a journal

Tianna’s archaeologist parents send her a battered journal from Bali. Its unfinished clues suggest that something is wrong. When they disappear, Tianna and her grandfather begin an international search connected to the disputed Beale ciphers.

Story boundary: This is promotional artwork for the fictional adventure. It does not claim that every image shown is a literal scene from the published book.

Historical boundary: The Beale Papers are real historical documents. The expedition, treasure claim and story behind them remain disputed.
