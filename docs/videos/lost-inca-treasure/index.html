Atahualpa’s ransom and legend

This Lost Treasure Is Worth Billions (And Still Missing)

The history behind Atahualpa’s ransom and the unverified legend of lost Inca treasure in the Llanganates Mountains.

Reviewed 12 September 2026

What the video explains

Atahualpa offered an enormous ransom after his capture at Cajamarca. Gold and silver that reached the Spanish were recorded, melted and divided. A later tradition says another cache was hidden in the Llanganates Mountains.

Evidence boundary: Atahualpa’s ransom is historical. A surviving royal cache in the Llanganates remains an unverified legend.
