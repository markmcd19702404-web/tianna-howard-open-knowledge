# Summary

A wartime art mystery inspired by the real Mask of a Faun, a Renaissance sculpture attributed to the young Michelangelo and missing since 1944.

# Historical basis

A Renaissance marble attributed to Michelangelo that was removed from Castle of Poppi during German looting in 1944.

# Evidence status
