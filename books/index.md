# Books

* [Tianna and the Mystery of the Beale Treasure](beale-treasure.md) - A fast-paced historical adventure inspired by the legendary Beale ciphers, following twelve-year-old Tianna Howard on an international trail of codes, danger and discovery.
* [Tianna and Michelangelo’s Mask of a Faun](mask-of-a-faun.md) - A wartime art mystery inspired by the real Mask of a Faun, a Renaissance sculpture attributed to the young Michelangelo and missing since 1944.
