# Definition

The Tianna Howard Adventures are historical fiction mysteries by M.L. Woodward for readers aged 9–13, combining real history and global locations with codes, hidden evidence and fictional investigations.

# Audience and genre

* Audience: Readers aged 9–13
* Genres: Historical fiction, Historical adventure, Mystery, Action and adventure
* Fantasy or magic: No

# Author

The series is written by [M.L. Woodward](/author/ml-woodward.md).

# Books in order

2. [Tianna and Michelangelo’s Mask of a Faun](/books/mask-of-a-faun.md)

# Accuracy boundary

The series is historical fiction. Real history supplies the foundation; Tianna's investigation and each story resolution are fictional.
