# Questions and answers

## Are the Tianna Howard Adventures based on true stories?

They are historical fiction inspired by real mysteries, people, places and events. Tianna, her investigations and the story resolutions are fictional.

## What age are the Tianna Howard Adventures for?

The books are written primarily for readers aged 11–14 and are positioned as upper middle grade.

## Are the books fantasy?

No. The adventures use real history and logical problem-solving without magic or supernatural powers.

## Who writes the Tianna Howard Adventures?

M.L. Woodward is the author of the Tianna Howard Adventures.
